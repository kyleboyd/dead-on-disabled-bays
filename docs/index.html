    About this data

    This site lists disabled parking bays in Belfast so Blue Badge holders can find one. It is a first version, built from public records.

    What to know before you rely on it

    • Nothing has been checked on the ground. On-street bays were read automatically from legal orders. A bay in an order may have been moved, faded or never marked.
    • Pin positions are worked out, not surveyed. Most were placed by measuring along the street from a junction, as the order describes. Bays marked “Approximate” could be further out. Bays with no pin are on the street named, but the exact spot is not known.
    • The number of spaces is an estimate based on the bay's length, unless the order gives a count.
    • Some bays are missing. The data covers the Parking Places (Disabled Persons' Vehicles) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and its amending orders up to S.R. 2026 No. 158. It leaves out 2026 Amendment Orders No. 4 and No. 5, and the Parking and Waiting Restrictions (Belfast) Order 2018, which may hold more city-centre bays.
    • “Belfast” means what the orders label Belfast. On-street bays listed under Dunmurry, Dundonald, Newtownabbey or Holywood are not included.
    • Council car parks: the council's dataset is undated. Where no Blue Badge count is shown, the dataset gives none; it does not mean there are no bays.
    • Hospitals and shopping centres: counts are as published by each operator and have no map position. Supermarkets, retail parks, stations and private car parks are not covered.

    Your privacy

    “Find bays near me” uses your location only inside your browser to measure distances. It is not sent to this site or stored. The map images are loaded from OpenFreeMap and the map software from unpkg.com, so those services see your request as they would for any web page. “Directions” links open Google Maps with the bay's position.
